# Elyramart Terms of Use (Draft)

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026  
**Operator:** Elyramart, Bangladesh  
**Contact:** elyramartbd@gmail.com

This draft is intended for owner and legal review before publication. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Platform role
Elyramart provides an online marketplace and home-service request platform. Products may be supplied by independent partner sellers. Service requests may be referred to independent technicians or service providers. The owner must accurately disclose the legal operator, seller identities, and provider relationships before launch.

## 2. Accounts
Users must provide accurate contact and delivery information, protect their sign-in credentials, and notify Elyramart if they suspect unauthorized account use. Users must not impersonate another person or misuse the platform.

## 3. Orders and payment
The current app supports cash on delivery only. An order request is not confirmed until Elyramart or the relevant seller confirms availability and delivery arrangements. Product availability, final payable amount, delivery charge, and estimated delivery time should be confirmed before dispatch. Do not send advance payment to an individual unless Elyramart publishes an authorized payment method and receipt process.

## 4. Sellers
Seller applications are subject to review and approval. Sellers are responsible for accurate product descriptions, lawful sourcing, stock availability, safe packaging, and compliance with applicable laws. Elyramart may reject, suspend, or remove listings that are inaccurate, unsafe, unlawful, or violate platform rules.

## 5. Home services
A service request is a request for contact, not a guaranteed appointment or fixed quotation. The provider should confirm scope, price, timing, and any parts or materials before work begins. Elyramart is not an emergency-response service. For urgent electrical, gas, fire, medical, or other immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service.

## 6. Restricted and regulated products
Users and sellers must not list unlawful, counterfeit, recalled, unsafe, or restricted products. Medicines and other regulated goods may only be listed, sold, stored, and delivered where the seller and transaction meet all applicable Bangladesh licensing, prescription, storage, and delivery requirements. Elyramart must not advertise itself as a pharmacy or healthcare provider unless legally authorized.

## 7. Cancellations, returns and complaints
Before public launch, Elyramart must publish clear category-specific cancellation, return, refund, damaged-item, and complaint procedures, including the responsible party and response timeframe. Nothing in these terms removes any non-waivable rights under applicable law.

## 8. Platform availability and misuse
Elyramart may temporarily suspend access for maintenance, security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Users must not interfere with platform security, submit false orders, scrape private information, or abuse sellers, technicians, or other customers.

## 9. Limitation and applicable law
These terms must be reviewed and adapted by a qualified Bangladesh legal professional to identify the correct contracting entity, consumer-rights obligations, liability allocation, dispute process, and governing-law wording. No limitation in this draft is intended to exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 10. Changes and contact
The published version will identify its effective date and explain material changes. Questions or complaints: elyramartbd@gmail.com.
