# Elyramart Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026

Elyramart is a marketplace and home-service platform operated by Elyramart. Contact: elyramartbd@gmail.com.

## Information we collect
When you create an account or place an order, the app may collect your name, email address, phone number, account identifier, delivery address, order contents, order status and seller application details. Authentication is provided by Firebase Authentication and marketplace records are stored in Cloud Firestore.

## How information is used
We use this information to create and secure accounts, process and deliver orders, communicate about orders, review seller applications, provide customer support, prevent misuse and operate the marketplace.

## Payments
The current app version supports cash on delivery only. It does not collect payment-card or mobile-wallet credentials. If online payment is added later, this policy must be updated before launch.

## Service providers
The app uses Google Firebase services for authentication, database and app infrastructure. Those services process information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

## Sharing
Order details may be shared with the relevant seller and delivery personnel to fulfil an order. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect users and the platform.

## Retention and deletion
We retain account and order information for as long as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. To request account or data deletion, contact elyramartbd@gmail.com from the email address associated with the account. Some transaction records may need to be retained where law requires.

## Security
We use Firebase Authentication and database access rules. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Access controls and rules must be reviewed and tested before public release.

## Children
Elyramart is not designed for children under 13. Children should not create accounts or submit personal information.

## Changes
We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a revised effective date.

## Contact
Email: elyramartbd@gmail.com

**Publication note:** This is a draft based on the current app implementation. The owner must review and confirm actual operational practices, legal identity, retention periods and applicable Bangladesh legal requirements before publishing or submitting to Google Play.
